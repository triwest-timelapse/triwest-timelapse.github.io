Timelapse

Construction timelapse recording for Windows.

Timelapse is a Windows application that takes scheduled snapshots from site cameras, turns them into timelapse videos, and keeps them organized by client, site and camera.

It runs entirely on the owner's own PC. When the owner chooses to, it can:

  • upload finished videos to the owner's Google Drive, and
  • stream a camera live to the owner's YouTube channel as an unlisted video.
