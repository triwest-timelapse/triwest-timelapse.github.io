Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy covers the Timelapse Windows application ("the app"). The app is used privately by its owner to record construction timelapses. It is not offered to the public.

Where your data lives

The app runs only on the PC it is installed on. Camera snapshots, videos, settings and sign-in tokens are stored on that PC. Nothing is sent to the app's developer or to any server other than the Google services listed below, and only when you connect them.

Google data the app uses

  • Google Drive (drive.file): the app uploads finished timelapse videos to your Drive and can create share links for them. It can only see and manage files it created itself. It cannot read your other Drive files.
  • YouTube (youtube): the app creates unlisted live broadcasts on your channel and streams a camera to them. It ends those broadcasts when you stop the stream. It does not read, change or delete your other videos, comments or account details.

The app uses this access only to provide those features. Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on your PC. Camera passwords and stream keys are stored the same way.

Removing access and data

You can disconnect Google Drive or YouTube at any time in the app's settings, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes everything it stored on your PC. Videos already uploaded stay in your Drive or YouTube account until you delete them there.
